Post-Tropical Cyclone Odalys Discussion Number  32
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL       EP162026
200 PM PDT Sun Sep 27 2026
 
Odalys has lacked organized deep convection for about 12 hours, and 
the system has been a swirl of low-level clouds all day. Therefore, 
Odalys no longer meets the criteria of a tropical cyclone, and this 
is the last advisory on this system. A recent ASCAT-B pass from a 
few hours ago showed peak winds between 35 and 40 kt, and based on 
that data, the initial wind speed is lowered to 40 kt.
 
Strong wind shear, dry air, and cool waters should cause continued
weakening while the system turns southward or south-southwestward
in the low-level flow. Dissipation is now shown by 72 hours, in
agreement with all of the global model guidance.
 
Distant moisture from both Odalys and Hurricane Polo will be
transported into the southwestern United States during the next few
days, and may indirectly contribute to a heavy rainfall and flash
flooding in the region. See products issued by the Weather
Prediction Center for more information.
