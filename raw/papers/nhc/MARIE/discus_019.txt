Hurricane Marie Discussion Number  19
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL       EP132026
200 PM PDT Sat Sep 05 2026
 
Marie continues to exhibit a large ragged eye, around 60 n mi in
diameter. Satellite estimates of the hurricane's intensity are very
similar to what they were 6 hours ago, and therefore the initial
intensity remains 80 kt for this advisory. The heading of
the hurricane has been similarly steady, albeit at a slightly 
slower forward speed, with a motion estimate of 305/6 kt.

Marie is moving along the western periphery of a ridge centered over 
the southern United States and will likely continue on a generally 
northwestward heading for most of the weekend. A short-term bend 
toward the west-northwest is still expected early next week as the 
cyclone weakens but it will ultimately accelerate northwestward as a 
mid- to upper-level trough digs quickly southward to the west of 
Marie by late Tuesday.

A drier environment and cooler SSTs are still expected to cause 
Marie to slowly weaken during the next couple of days. The rate of 
weakening could increase after that as the environment gets more 
hostile and the aforementioned trough causes an increase in 
southerly wind shear across the cyclone. Marie is still expected to 
become a post-tropical remnant low near the 96 h mark, and could 
dissipate in about 5 days, though the forecast conservatively shows 
a point at that time for now. The NHC track and intensity forecasts 
are very close to the HCCA and GDM consensus aids.
