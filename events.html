Event log

Every notable storm, flood, heatwave and climate driver we follow, newest first. Each entry explains the setup, the trigger, what amplified it, what steered it and how it ended. Entries marked unfolding are updated as the event develops; open means not enough information has been published yet; summary means a full deep-dive is still to come.

Data snapshot: 16 October 2026. Independent, non-commercial analysis built from public sources (NOAA, NASA, USGS, Smithsonian GVP, GDACS, CPC, IRI, Copernicus/Open-Meteo, SILSO, IERS, Coral Reef Watch and others; see About & method). Not an official forecast or warning service — for warnings follow your national weather service. Analysis prepared with Claude (Anthropic).
