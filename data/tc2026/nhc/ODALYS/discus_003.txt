Tropical Storm Odalys Discussion Number   3
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL       EP162026
200 PM PDT Sun Sep 20 2026
 
Odalys continues to gradually become better organized, with very 
deep convection near and over the center, and some banding features 
evident over the southern semicircle.  Upper-level outflow remains 
somewhat restricted over the northern quadrant of the circulation, 
however.  This implies some northerly vertical wind shear over the 
storm.  The estimated current intensity is increased to 45 kt based 
on objective and subjective Dvorak estimates and observations from 
a scatterometer overpass.

The tropical cyclone is expected to be in an environment of 
low to moderate shear, over very warm waters, and within a moist 
low- to mid-level air mass for the next couple of days.  Therefore 
continued strengthening is forecast for the next couple of days.  
Later in the forecast period, somewhat drier air and the possible 
inhibiting influence of a developing tropical cyclone to the east 
could limit strengthening.  The official intensity forecast is 
close to the model consensus.

Odalys should move generally northwestward through tonight, on the 
southwest side of a mid-level ridge.  In 24-48 hours, a 500 mb 
trough moving into California and Baja California should cause the 
system to turn eastward.  Later in the forecast period, as the 
trough lifts out, Odalys is likely to move northeastward on the 
northwestern side of a mid-level ridge.  The developing tropical 
cyclone to the east could also have some influence on the track in 
3-5 days, although there is considerable uncertainty about this.
