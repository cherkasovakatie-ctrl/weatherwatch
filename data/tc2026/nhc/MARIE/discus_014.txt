Hurricane Marie Discussion Number  14
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL       EP132026
800 AM PDT Fri Sep 04 2026
 
Deep convection is concentrated south and east of a ragged eye, 
while dry air continues to wrap around the northern and western 
portions of the circulation. A recent GPM GMI microwave pass showed 
a slightly elongated eye along with erosion of the western eyewall. 
The latest Dvorak intensity estimate from TAFB was 5.0/90 kt, while 
remotely-sensed estimates span a broader range of 69-96 kt. The 
initial intensity is maintained at 85 kt for this advisory. 
 
Marie is moving toward the west-northwest around 8 kt, and is 
flanked by a weak mid-level ridge to the west and a strong closed 
low moving into the Pacific Northwest. The hurricane is expected to 
continue on this heading through tonight before the ridge weakens 
and the low digs southeastward over California, causing Marie to 
turn northwestward. Little change has been made to the previous 
forecast through days 3-4. After day 4, the weakening cyclone is 
expected to become increasingly steered by the low- to mid-level 
environmental flow and remain well offshore over the eastern 
Pacific. The official track forecast has been shifted to the right 
of the previous advisory beyond day 4, toward the latest Google 
DeepMind and corrected consensus (HCCA) guidance.

Despite moving over warm waters and through an environment of low 
wind shear, Marie is contending with relatively dry mid-level air, 
which is likely limiting significant strengthening. Similar 
environmental conditions are expected through tonight, supporting 
little change in intensity. Beginning Saturday, Marie is forecast to 
encounter increasingly dry low- and mid-level air along with cooler 
sea surface temperatures, resulting in gradual weakening. More 
substantial weakening is expected next week as the hurricane moves 
into increasingly unfavorable conditions. The official forecast is 
close to the intensity model consensus.
