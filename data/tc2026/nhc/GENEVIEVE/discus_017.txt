Hurricane Genevieve Discussion Number  17
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL       EP072026
800 AM PDT Tue Jul 28 2026
 
The eye of Genevieve has become slightly ragged and is wobbling this 
morning, and the temperature in the eye is now below 0 C. An earlier 
SSMIS pass over the hurricane showed the affects of weak northerly 
wind shear and some erosion of the structure in the northwest 
quadrant. However, colder eyewall cloud top temperatures are 
wrapping back around the northern and western side of the eye, due 
to an uptick in convection as noted on first light visible satellite 
imagery, indicating that a small fluctuation in intensity may be 
occurring over the past hour or so. The subjective Dvorak intensity 
estimate from TAFB was 6.0/115 kt, while the objective estimates 
range from 96-114 kt. The initial intensity is set at 110 kt for 
this advisory. 

Genevieve is moving toward the west-northwest, or 300/8 kt, guided 
by a stout mid and upper-level ridge centered over the southwestern 
United States. A slight increase in forward speed is forecast later 
this week as the ridge expands westward, and a strong trough 
approaches the west coast of the U.S. The latest NHC forecast lies 
within the tightly clustered guidance, and is closest to HCCA and 
the Google DeepMind solutions. 

Small fluctuations in intensity will be possible today. The 
Microwave-based Probability of Eyewall Replacement Cycle (MPERC) 
guidance indicates a 33 percent chance of an eyewall replacement 
cycle over the next 24 h. However, a gradual weakening trend is 
expected during the remainder of the week as the hurricane moves 
into cooler waters and encounters drier mid-level air. On Thursday, 
Genevieve is forecast to cross north of 20N into an area with cool 
waters less than 26 C, and more pronounced weakening will commence 
and continue into this weekend. The NHC intensity forecast lies near 
the middle of the guidance envelope, and calls for Genevieve to fall 
below hurricane strength in a couple of days and become 
post-tropical this weekend.
