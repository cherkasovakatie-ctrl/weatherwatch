Hurricane Karina Discussion Number  29
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL       EP112026
500 AM HST Thu Sep 03 2026
 
Little has changed in Karina's annular appearance over the last
several hours. The latest subjective Dvorak analysis from TAFB gave
an intensity estimate of 102 kt, while objective estimates from
CIMSS ranged from 90-95 kt. Given the continued downward trend
across subjective and objective Dvorak analysis, the initial
intensity estimate was set at 100 kt.
 
Karina's initial motion was set at 280/9 kt. The system is forecast
to shift more west-northwest over the next couple of days, as it
continues to be guided by a subtropical ridge to its north. By
late this weekend into early next week, an oncoming upper-level
trough is expected to pull Karina more to the northwest, away from
the main Hawaiian Islands. The official track forecast was little
changed from the previous advisory, with only slight adjustments
westward to reflect a blend of HCCA, GDMI, and the previous
official track forecast.
 
A steady weakening trend in Karina's intensity is still expected to
occur as the system encounters an increasingly drier mid-level
environment. After 24 h, Karina's weakening trend is
forecast to hasten as vertical wind shear increases and SSTs
decrease, with the system expected to become post-tropical by Day
4. The official intensity forecast increases the rate of weakening
compared to the previous advisory, falling just above most model
guidance within the first 24 h before landing closer to guidance
consensus thereafter.
