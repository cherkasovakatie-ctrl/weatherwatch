How the 1997–98 El Niño developed

Planned Paper 2 of 4 · Research date: 18 October 2026 · Observational cutoff: historical event · Literature: open, updated as new studies appear · Primary index: RONI (NOAA CPC)

Status. Planned: work starts after the 1982–83 paper is finalised.

Planned scope

This paper will follow the same framework as Paper 1: preconditions, triggers, amplifiers, suppressors, feedbacks and consequences, each graded A–D on event-specific evidence, with a timeline, an evidence table and verified references. RONI is the primary index.

Questions specific to this event:

  • The first event observed by the completed TAO/TRITON array and satellite altimetry: how much better resolved is the onset than in 1982?
  • The sequence of strong westerly wind events from late 1996 to mid-1997 and their Kelvin waves.
  • Why it rose so fast from a weak La Niña start (RONI peak +2.28, NDJ 1997).
  • How well it was forecast in real time, and its abrupt mid-1998 termination.
